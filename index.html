The idea

A ward where no one is treated as an average.

Every patient arrives with their own normal: a usual heart rate, a usual way of breathing, a usual pace of recovery. A rule built for the average patient may miss the person who is quietly changing, and keep sounding for the person who is simply being themselves.

We want to help nurses and doctors with that individuality without reading every chart line by line, so the right person gets attention at the right moment, and each patient is treated as themselves.

Clinicians first

The nurse and the doctor are the centre of the system.

Basalphy does not diagnose, decide or act. It notices and explains. You judge, because only you can truly see the person in front of you.

Basalphy

Notices

Learns each patient's usual and notify meaningful changes, with the evidence behind it.

The Core Driver

Nurses and doctors' decision

Weigh against what no system can record: the person, the context, their story. Whether to confirm, dismiss, or escalate it, your judgement matter.

The patient

Being treated as themselves

Care is shaped around the individual's own baseline, not only the population average.

How it works

Three steps, designed around your judgement.

This is what we are designing toward. The product is not built yet.

1

Learn the baseline

From each patient's own records and monitoring data, Basalphy builds a picture of what normal looks like for that person.

2

Notice what changed

When something moves outside that person's usual pattern, it is surfaced: what changed, by how much, and over how long.

3

Explain, then hand over

Every flag comes with its reasons and its uncertainty. A clinician confirms or dismisses it, and that feedback is meant to cut the noise over time.

Our principles

Built to earn a clinician's trust.

Support, never replace

Basalphy is decision support. A person makes every clinical decision.

Every alert explains itself

A flag you cannot question is a flag you cannot trust. We show the evidence behind each one.

Fewer, better alerts

An ignored alert helps no one, and a false alarm can pull attention from another bed. We design for alert quality before alert quantity.

Honest about uncertainty

Basalphy shows how confident it is, and says so plainly when it does not know.

Where we are

At the start, listening.

Basalphy is a concept. We have not built the product, it is not a medical device, and it is not yet for clinical use. We would rather build it with the people who will use it.

Now

Listen

Talk with nurses and doctors about which changes in a patient they would want noticed sooner.

Next

Test on past data

Check the approach on de-identified historical records before it touches a live ward.

Then

Supervised pilot

One ward, clinicians in control, and regulatory review before any clinical use.

Later

Beyond one hospital

Work across different hospital record systems without rebuilding for each one. Our wish is to be the bridge between the current hospital system, future technology improvement, and medical professionals as the center.

Research foundation: this builds on published work on schema-agnostic models of electronic health records, such as GenHPF (Hur et al., 2022).
